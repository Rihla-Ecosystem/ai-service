## Advises against ALL travel
- Within 20km of the Egypt-Libya border (except El Salloum town, see below)
- North Sinai Governorate

## Advises against all BUT ESSENTIAL travel
- El Salloum town
- Northern part of South Sinai Governorate, beyond the St Catherine-Nuweibaa
  road (coastal strips on both sides of the peninsula excluded from this -
  i.e. still fine to visit)
- Ismailiyah Governorate, east of the Suez Canal
- Hala'ib Triangle and Bir Tawil Trapezoid
- Area west of the Nile Valley and Nile Delta, EXCEPT the explicit safe
  corridors listed below

## Explicit Western Desert safe corridors (confirmed verbatim pattern from gov.uk)
- Luxor, Qina, Aswan, Abu Simbel, Valley of the Kings
- The road between Giza and Farafra, plus 50km either side of that road
- FCDO specifically still advises against all but essential travel on the
  Bahariya-Siwa desert road (this is the one exception carved back out)

## Context note
As of the FCDO's most recent update (referenced March 2026), the core zone

FCDO added general "heightened regional risk" language rather than new
zone restrictions.
