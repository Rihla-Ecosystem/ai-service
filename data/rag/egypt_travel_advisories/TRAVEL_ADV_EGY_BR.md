## Baseline status
"Alto grau de cautela" (high degree of caution) - Brazil does not use a
numbered tier system like the US; it's a narrative advisory.

## Do Not Travel / avoid entirely
- North Sinai
- Western Desert areas near the Libyan border

## Important distinction
Egypt was NOT included in Itamaraty's March 2026 emergency list of 11
Middle Eastern countries Brazilians were told to avoid entirely during the
Iran-Israel-US escalation (that list: Iran, Israel, Qatar, Kuwait, UAE,
Bahrain, Jordan, Iraq, Lebanon, Palestine, Syria). Egypt has stayed on the
standing "high caution" tier rather than an acute avoidance tier.

## General guidance during regional tension
- Follow local authority instructions, avoid protests/large gatherings
- Monitor Brazilian embassy communications and local media
- Keep travel documents valid 6+ months
- Register with consulate contact info before travel
