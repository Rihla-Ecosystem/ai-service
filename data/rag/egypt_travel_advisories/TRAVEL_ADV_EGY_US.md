## Baseline status
Level 2: Exercise Increased Caution (4-tier scale, 1=lowest risk, 4=highest).
Confirmed current as of embassy alerts through April 2026.

## Do Not Travel zones (hard restriction within the Level 2 country rating)
- Northern and Middle Sinai Peninsula
- Parts of the Western Desert (bordering Libya)

## Confirmed operational detail
- Sinai/Taba land crossing: a 30-day visa on arrival costs $30 USD cash, plus
  $20-25 USD cash for a "letter of guarantee" from an authorized travel agent,
  required if traveling beyond Sinai into mainland Egypt (Cairo, Hurghada,
  etc.) or flying out via Sharm El-Sheikh/Cairo. (Confirmed via US Embassy
  Cairo security alert, March 2026 - note this contradicts an earlier
  unverified "$120" figure circulating elsewhere, which appears fabricated.)
- Travelers near Taba are encouraged to enroll in Israel's Home Front Command
  alert system due to regional missile-targeting risk.

## Cause codes cited

broader region noted as elevated risk context (not Egypt-specific attack).
